Features
Based on the fourth-generation LoRa multi-band transceiver LR2021
Supports Sub-GHz (410 ~ 510 MHz / 850 ~ 930 MHz), 2.4 GHz ISM band, and 1.5 ~ 2.5 GHz licensed bands
Connects to the cloud via LoRa or LoRaWAN protocol through a gateway, enabling low-power wide-area networking (LPWAN)
Compatible with multiple protocols including LoRa, FLRC, FSK, BLE, and ZigBee
SPI communication interface, supports mainstream MCU platforms for seamless integration and migration
Suitable for IoT applications such as industrial telemetry, smart home, environmental monitoring, and remote data acquisition
Comes with online development resources and manual (examples for ESP32 / Raspberry Pi / Arduino / STM32 / Raspberry Pi Pico)

Strong Communication Compatibility
The LR2021 can communicate with LR1121, SX126X, SX127X, and SX128X series, maintaining backward compatibility with existing LoRa products after upgrades, and provides higher power efficiency and longer transmission distance.
Dual Spread Spectrum Modulation for Complex Environments
The LR2021 integrates both LoRa Chirp Spread Spectrum and Long Range – Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum technologies, supports Sub-GHz, 2.4 GHz ISM band, and 1.5 ~ 2.5 GHz licensed tri-band coverage, enabling both terrestrial IoT and satellite IoT communication requirements. It is suitable for applications in industrial, agricultural, smart city, and satellite IoT scenarios.
