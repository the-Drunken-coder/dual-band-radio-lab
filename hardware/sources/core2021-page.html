Core2021-XF

  • Core2021-HF (Standard version)
  • Core2021-LF (Standard version)

This product uses the Semtech LR2021 long-range ultra-low-power multi-band LoRa® transceiver, supporting Sub-GHz (150–960 MHz), the 2.4 GHz ISM band, and the 1.5–2.5 GHz licensed band, meeting the requirements of both terrestrial wireless communication and satellite communication applications. It is compatible with multiple modulation methods such as LoRa®, LoRaWAN®, (G)FSK, FLRC, and LR-FHSS, making it suitable for LPWAN, smart home, industrial IoT, asset tracking, and satellite IoT scenarios. The LR2021 adopts the fourth-generation LoRa® IP architecture, offering higher receive sensitivity, lower power consumption, and more flexible multi-region single-SKU design capabilities.

Features​

  • Uses the fourth-generation LoRa® multi-band transceiver LR2021 from Semtech
  • Supports Sub-GHz (410 ~ 510MHz / 850 ~ 930MHz), the 2.4 GHz ISM band, and the 1.5 ~ 2.5 GHz licensed band
  • Connects to the cloud via LoRa or LoRaWAN protocols through a gateway, building a low-power wide-area network
  • Compatible with multiple protocols such as LoRa, FLRC, FSK, BLE, and ZigBee
  • SPI control, supports mainstream MCU platforms for easy integration and porting
  • Suitable for IoT applications such as industrial telemetry, smart homes, environmental monitoring, and remote data acquisition
  • Provides comprehensive supporting documentation and manuals (example programs and user manuals for ESP32, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, STM32, and Raspberry Pi Pico, etc.)
